How it works

Data comes in, FLEo1 decides what to compute and SINcopi solves. The answer comes out as MW bands, with the basis stated.

Method diagram: public and private data sources enter a manifest; FLEo1 selects case, load level, cut, scenarios, contingencies, model and solver; SINcopi solves with n-1 screening over the MW ladder, compared with open models and with the official chain as reference, and produces a band, a verdict and a robustness.

01

Data

Public and private sources enter the same manifest: the ONS DESSEM deck, open generation, load and curtailment data, ANEEL's SIGA register and SIGEL substations, EPE and IBGE geometry, ERA5 weather. Every file is recorded with its hash and vintage before it becomes a network. A customer's private data, such as a utility's own network or a generator's contracts, follows the same path.

02

FLEo1

FLEo1, our AI model, orchestrates the case. From the question it decides which model to run and with which data: the date and load level, the voltage and region cut, the hydrological scenarios, the contingency set, the MW ladder and the solver. Every decision is recorded in the case, and it is what the final screen declares as its basis.

03

SINcopi

SINcopi solves the DC power flow with n-1 screening over the Rede Básica, climbs the MW ladder with warm starts and tests the verdict's sensitivity to changes in ratings and load. Open market models such as PyPSA, pandapower and PowerModels.jl run on the same case for comparison, and the official NEWAVE, DECOMP and DESSEM chain enters as the reference. The answer comes out as an MW band, a verdict and a robustness, never as a point.

Results

01

Generation

Screen · Generation map

Where the grid curtails your generation, and how much. The map crosses solar and wind potential, transmission lines and the curtailment ONS records per cluster, month by month; the causes ONS declares, electrical, reliability, energy or access opinion, enter as the screening's vocabulary. It is the basis for curtailment scenarios and for PPA planning.

02

Transmission & Distribution

Screen · Inquiry Desk

An access inquiry arrives and the utility screens its own network against the requested load: which buses serve it, in what band, how robustly, and which constraints it names itself. A flexible load enters as a filter of the same screening. The answer leaves as a pack, basis stated, no reinforcement priced.

03

Data Centers

Screen · Atlas

Where a large new load fits today on the Rede Básica, in what band and how robustly. Each substation gets a verdict per MW step, a headroom band and a time class; when the model cannot say, it withholds and states why. It is speed-to-power measured at scale, with the basis stated on the screen itself: screening, not an access study.
