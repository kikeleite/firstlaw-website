What the battery does

  1. 01

    Cuts peak demand

    It discharges during the three peak hours. Measured demand drops, you contract less and stop paying overrun charges.

  2. 02

    Buys cheap, uses at peak

    It charges off-peak and gives the energy back at peak. On the Green tariff, every MWh moved out of the peak stops paying the utility's peak tariff. In the free market, add the price difference of each hour, according to your contract.

  3. 03

    Keeps the plant running when the grid goes down

    While the generator starts, the battery carries the load. No line stoppage.
